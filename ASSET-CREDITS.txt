Distant oak/alder images are offline Blender Cycles renders of the supplied
models. Original models are retained for close views. Static transforms and
material batches are derived in memory, without replacing original GLB bytes.

The six Lost Warrior uploads exceeded the tool's 32 MiB transfer limit and
could not be downloaded/inspected. No Lost Warrior geometry or animations are
claimed to be present. The Mixamo character above remains for player and NPCs;
its original Idle/Walk/Run clips are blended. Attack, guard, draw/sheath and
aerial poses are procedural offsets, not newly supplied motion-capture clips.

V4 returns to the photographic grass texture from the Three.js examples
examples/textures/terrain/grasslight-big.jpg; sRGB color maps and linear normal
map data are treated separately. All original photo-surface credits above apply.
New terrain blending, grass geometry, weather/sky/water shader extensions,
quests, combat, interfaces and procedural audio were created for Northreach.

V4.1 LOCAL CHARACTER IMPORT AND GRAPHICS
The five new Lost Warrior RAR uploads again exceed the chat transfer tool's
32 MiB limit; their contents were not available for inspection here. The game
now provides a local-only character importer for the player's own FBX, animation
and texture archives/folders. No Lost Warrior geometry is claimed to be bundled
before that import. Imported models remain on the player's computer/browser.

RAR decoding: node-unrar-js 2.0.2 by Jianrong Yu (MIT wrapper; license included).
https://github.com/YuJianrong/node-unrar.js
Its WebAssembly decoder uses the UnRAR library. The UnRAR source license is
included as UNRAR-LICENSE.txt; it is used only for decompression, not RAR creation.
License text source: https://github.com/pmachapman/unrar/blob/master/license.txt
Three.js FBX/OBJ/MTL/TGA/DDS loaders, SkeletonUtils and GLTFExporter are covered by
the included Three.js license. Source-model rights continue to apply to imports.

The blue humanoid used for import regression tests is an authored synthetic
fixture, not Lost Warrior. It is not bundled as the game's default character.
Rounded landscape rocks, the outdoor sky-light probe and contact-shadow texture
are procedural. Tree impostors were re-rendered at 512x512 with uncropped framing.
